      THE BIG SCREEN

      Set the room in motion.

      Connect your laptop to your Samsung display, then move this browser window onto that screen.

      1. Give it all the pixels.For your Samsung Follow Me Pro 43″, set the connected computer’s display rotation to 90° or 270° and select 2160 × 3840 portrait output at 60 Hz when available. Rotate the screen to match. Artwork stays upright; ArtDrops does not rotate your desktop.
      2. Make your selection.Choose “Portrait screen · 9:16,” then “Build a 1-hour mix.” The selection uses high-resolution upright works that fill at least 90% of your screen without cropping. Native 4K portraits are labeled; other works are displayed at their available resolution. “Between artworks” lets you choose a gentle fade or an instant switch.
      3. Press play. Settle in.Start the exhibition and press F for fullscreen. Keep the laptop plugged in and disable system sleep for a long show. Use the laptop browser over HDMI or USB-C and keep an internet connection for artwork playback. The Samsung’s built-in browser has not been verified for this show.
      ←→ Change workSpace PauseF FullscreenEsc Leave show

      Playback is silent. The one-hour mix reshuffles and repeats for a four-hour event, or as long as you leave it running. Close the exhibition to stop it.

      A NOTE ON THE COLLECTION

      Artists first.

      A saved library of moving art, photographs, and paintings from public catalogs and your chosen references. It’s an introduction to the artists, not a sales ranking.

      Every work links to its source. ArtDrops does not own or sell the artwork. A checked link can still become unavailable, so the player skips failed works and keeps the show going.

      Public exhibition permissions depend on the artwork and artist. Check the source or contact the artist before including their work in your public show.
