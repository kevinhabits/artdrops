    THE BIG SCREEN

    Set the room in motion.

    Connect your laptop to your Samsung display, then move this browser window onto that screen.

    1. Give it all the pixels.Choose 3840 × 2160 in your computer’s display settings when supported. Artwork plays at its available source resolution.
    2. Make your selection.Add works, adjust the order, and choose the time with each piece. “Show full artwork” preserves the original composition.
    3. Press play. Settle in.Start the exhibition and press F for fullscreen. Keep the laptop plugged in and disable system sleep for a long show. An internet connection is needed for artwork playback.
    ←→ Change workSpace PauseF FullscreenEsc Leave show

    Playback is silent. Your exhibition repeats until you close it.

    A NOTE ON THE COLLECTION

    Artists first. Motion always.

    This is a saved selection of moving artworks from public marketplace catalogs, chosen for variety and playable media. It’s an introduction to the artists, not a sales ranking.

    Every work links to its source. The art streams from its original host; ArtDrops does not own or sell it. A checked link can still become unavailable, so the player skips failed works and keeps the show going.

    Public exhibition permissions depend on the artwork and artist. Check the source or contact the artist before including their work in your public show.
